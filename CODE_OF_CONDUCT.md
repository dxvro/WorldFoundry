# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want WorldFoundry to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## Our standards

Good behavior includes:

- being kind and patient, especially with newcomers
- respecting different opinions and experiences
- giving and accepting helpful criticism
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Not accepted:

- insults, harassment, trolling or personal attacks
- sexualized language or images
- publishing someone's private information without their permission
- any other behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to <worldfoundry0@gmail.com>. All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. The maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions, and may ban contributors, temporarily or permanently, for behavior they consider inappropriate.
